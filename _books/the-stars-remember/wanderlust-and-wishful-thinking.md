As old wounds begin to heal and new dreams take root, peace finally seems within reach.

At the Damselfly Inn, one couple prepares for a new chapter while confronting ghosts from the past. Across the bay in Sausalito, another has traded impossible adventures for an ordinary life they never thought they'd have.

Until a stranger appears with a plea no one else can answer.

One decision. One impossible choice. And consequences that could echo across worlds.
