When Dylan arrives at the Damselfly Inn in Mendocino, California, two very different women find themselves drawn to the same enigmatic man from their past. What begins as an ordinary love triangle slowly unravels into something neither friendship nor romance can explain.

Just when everyone believes their paths have finally become clear, Dylan makes an unexpected request that will send one woman far beyond anything she ever imagined possible.
