Summoned to San Francisco by the one man she never quite forgot, Wanderlust discovers that Dylan has been hiding a truth no human should be able to believe.

Together they embark on a journey across Mongolia in search of a mysterious child whose existence may determine the fate of more than one universe.

But saving the future may require sacrificing the very life they once dreamed of together, creating more questions than answers.
