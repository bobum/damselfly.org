Grace Bennett has finally begun to build a life in Willowbrook—a fragile, hopeful thing she guards with quiet gratitude. But when a face from her past reappears and a long-kept secret threatens to unravel everything, she is forced to confront the one wound she never let heal.

As spring rains swell the river toward the old ford where it all began, Grace must choose between the safety of resentment and the terrifying freedom of forgiveness. *Rivers of Grace* is a story about the cost of letting go and the wideness of mercy—how it carries us further than we ever meant to go, and delivers us somewhere better than we could have planned.
