Where the road out of Willowbrook dips down to meet the water, the old river ford has carried the town's comings and goings for generations. Long before the highway bridge was built, wagons and travelers crossed here at the shallow bend, and the smooth stones still show the wear of a hundred years of passage. To the people of Willowbrook the ford is more than a crossing—it is a threshold, the place where the town ends and the wider world begins, and where more than one life has turned on a single decision made at the water's edge. When the spring rains come and the river rises, the ford disappears beneath the current, and old secrets long hidden in its silt have a way of rising with it. For Grace, the ford is where it all began and where, at last, she must decide whether to let the waters carry her wounds away.
