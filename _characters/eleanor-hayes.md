Eleanor Hayes has lived in Willowbrook her whole life and knows every family's joys and sorrows the way she knows her own hymnal. Widowed twice and unbowed, she keeps the church flowers, the town's oldest recipes, and a running list of everyone in need of prayer—whether they've asked for it or not.

To Grace, Eleanor is at first an unwelcome reminder of everything she left behind. But Eleanor's blunt affection and stubborn hope prove impossible to resist. She becomes the series' quiet prophet, the one who keeps insisting—against all evidence—that grace is never wasted and no one is ever truly beyond coming home.
