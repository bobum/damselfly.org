Grace left Willowbrook the summer she turned nineteen, certain that distance was the only cure for the grief and guilt she carried. She built a capable, guarded life in the city, the kind that leaves no room for old wounds to ache. But beneath her composure runs a deep current of longing—for belonging, for forgiveness, for a faith she once knew and lost.

Her return to Willowbrook forces Grace to face the people and places she spent years avoiding. Slowly, reluctantly, she learns that the strength she prized was often just fear wearing armor, and that the grace she'd written off as childish may be the truest thing she's ever known. Grace's journey is the heart of the series: a portrait of a woman learning, one halting step at a time, how to come home.
