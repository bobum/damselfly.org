Thomas knows what it is to lose. Years after burying his wife, he pours himself into his work—mending porches, restoring pews, keeping the old chapel from falling to ruin—as a way of keeping his hands busy and his heart from wandering too far into sorrow. He is a man of few words and deep convictions, the sort who shows love by showing up.

When Grace returns to Willowbrook, Thomas offers her something she didn't know she needed: a presence that neither pushes nor flinches. Their slow, careful friendship becomes one of the quiet miracles of the series, a reminder that healing often comes not in grand gestures but in the patient, faithful company of someone willing to stay.
