At the heart of the series are four unforgettable people.

**Wendy**—a thoughtful innkeeper whose quiet strength hides a heart learning that sometimes the greatest act of love is letting go.

**Ashe**, affectionately nicknamed *"Wanderlust,"* a free-spirited investigative journalist whose curiosity has always pulled her toward impossible stories...and impossible people.

**Dylan**, a charming software engineer and photographer carrying a secret so extraordinary it could unravel everything either woman believes about reality.

**Cole**, a steady soul whose own fractured past may hold the key to building the future they've all been searching for.

As friendships deepen, loyalties shift, and impossible choices reshape every relationship, one discovery changes everything: the universe is far stranger—and far more fragile—than anyone ever imagined.
