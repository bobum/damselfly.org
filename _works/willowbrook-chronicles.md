Grace Bennett had spent fifteen years building a life far from Willowbrook, and she intended to keep it that way. But when a phone call in the middle of the night draws her back to the town she fled, she finds herself face to face with everything she tried to outrun—the old chapel on the hill, the river ford where she once made a promise, and the people who never stopped praying for her return.

What begins as a brief, reluctant visit becomes a season of unexpected grace. As Grace reconnects with her estranged family and an old friend she never forgot, she discovers that healing rarely arrives on our schedule, and that home is less a place than the people willing to welcome us back. The *Willowbrook Chronicles* trace her journey from wounded self-reliance to a hard-won, tender faith—and the quiet miracles that meet her along the way.
